AUTHORITATIVE INTERPRETATION
----------------------------
- rooms and pathways are the lower elevation plane
- surrounding terrain is an explicit higher plane
- walls are cliff faces at height transitions, not automatic closures around floor masks
- the higher plane may continue beyond a crop boundary without a south/east closing wall
- blank ASCII space alone never implies either elevated terrain or void; logical terrain data is explicit
- cliff faces descend toward the lower side
- freestanding raised platforms remain a separate later treatment
